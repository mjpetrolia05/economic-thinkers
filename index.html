The interrogation

Three questions for every suspect

Each file follows the same line of questioning, so you can compare thinkers answer by answer.

i.

What will we make?

Food or factories? Goods for now or machines for later? Can a society ever make too much?

ii.

How will we make it?

Who supplies what (labor, tools, land), and what counts as contributing to production?

iii.

How will we share it?

Wages, profits, rent: who gets which slice, and on whose authority?

When a powerful thinker makes an obvious error of logic, ask whose side they're on.
The working method of these files, paraphrasing Hunt & Lautzenheiser
