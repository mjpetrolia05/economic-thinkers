Food is 🦌 game, 🌾 grain or 🐄 cattle — any mix. Each holding on the rolled number yields its land's material; extra population raises your labour yield. To stockpile for a build, pick the SAVE option in the sharing phase — you keep the whole season's produce, at the cost of growth (and some unrest once you have a government).

Scoring

Stage ×3 · holdings 1 each (up to 6) · estate +1 · city +2 · luxury +1 · every 3 retainers +1 · every rule learned +1 · every 2 champion levels +1. Reach 30 points by season 24 and you win outright. If the seasons run out first, the richest society wins. Rivals cannot end the game early.
